    JetCo-UI Guide¶

    🎉 Welcome to the JetCo-UI Guide!

    Buckle up and get ready to dive into the world of JetCo-UI, where powerful, flexible components meet user-friendly design. Whether you're a beginner or a seasoned pro, this guide will serve as your ultimate roadmap to mastering the art of creating stunning UI elements with JetCo-UI.

    This guide is your go-to for everything JetCo-UI offers. Here's what you'll find:

    • 🧩 Components: Get to know the building blocks of JetCo-UI and how to assemble them into slick, responsive UIs.
    • 📝 Parameters: Learn how to fine-tune every aspect of your components, from colors and sizes to interactions.
    • 🛠️ Data Classes: Discover the data structures that power your UI, enabling smooth state management and dynamic content.
    • 💡 Usages: Need a quick reference or example? We've got real-world usage examples to guide you through every step.
    • 🎨 Customizations: Want to add your own flair? No problem! We'll show you how to tweak and personalize everything to match your vision.
    • ⚙️ Defaults & Utilities: Tap into sensible defaults to get started quickly, or use JetCo-UI's utilities to supercharge your workflow.

    Whether you're looking to explore the basics or push the limits with customizations, this guide has got your back! 🚀

    📊 Available Components¶

    JetCo-UI offers a comprehensive suite of UI components designed for modern Android applications:

    📈 Charts & Data Visualization¶

    • Pie Chart - Beautiful pie charts with customizable segments and animations
    • Column Bar Chart - Versatile bar charts for data comparison
    • Extended Column Bar Chart - Advanced bar charts with enhanced features
    • Group Column Bar Chart - Multi-series bar charts for complex data sets

    🎟️ Cards & Layouts¶

    • TicketCard - Ticket-style cards with cutout notches and dashed dividers
    • TicketContent - Layout helper for organizing ticket content

    🔄 Progress & Navigation¶

    • VerticalStepper - Vertical timeline/stepper with images and detailed descriptions
    • HorizontalStepper - Clean horizontal stepper for progress indication
    • CompactHorizontalStepper - Space-efficient horizontal stepper for mobile

    🎯 Component Categories¶

    Charts & Analytics 📊¶

    Perfect for displaying data, analytics, and metrics in your applications. All chart components support: - ✨ Smooth animations and transitions - 🎨 Complete visual customization - 📱 Responsive design for all screen sizes - ⚡ High performance with large datasets

    Cards & Containers 🎟️¶

    Specialized container components for unique layouts and designs: - 🎫 Authentic ticket-style designs - ✂️ Cutout effects and dashed dividers - 🎨 Gradient and solid color support - 📐 Flexible corner radius configurations

    Navigation & Progress 🔄¶

    Interactive components for guiding users through multi-step processes: - 📋 Multiple status states (Active, Complete, Error, Idle) - 🎭 Custom icons and imagery support - ⚡ Smooth progress animations - 👆 Interactive step navigation - 📱 Mobile-optimized touch targets
